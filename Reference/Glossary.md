## Campaign NPCs

- **Bill (William) Butterbur**: Bree merchant, head of the Greenway Trading Company, councilman; the party's employer · Session 1 [00:01:52] · Well respected; nearly all trade in and out of Bree goes through his company. A contact of the Rangers. Paid the party a 20 gp retainer each; seems to know more than he says. Brother of Barliman. Session 2 [00:39:00]: briefed the party in the Prancing Pony's parlour and lent them mounts. His company's seal bears **seven stars** aligned exactly like those in the barrow spirit's vision [02:48:26]. · *heard as:* Butterburry, Butterberg, Butterburk, Butterbird
- **Barliman Butterbur**: keeper of the Prancing Pony; Bill's brother · Session 1 [00:54:15] · Housed the party for the night. Session 2 [00:44:34]: provided the borrowed horses and ponies from the Pony's stables. · *heard as:* Barleman, Barleyman, Barlamen, Barlin, Bowden
- **Aldrin, son of Borin**: Ranger · Session 1 [00:22:18] · Thalion's teacher, companion and friend. Declined Bill's invitation and sent Thalion in his place. His lesson: "answers rarely wait where we left them" (Session 2 [00:27:00]). · *heard as:* Aldrin, son of Lorne, son of Borne
- **Odo Hornblower**: Nihla's brother · Session 1 [00:13:24] · Chosen by the family for the Bree business; once lost an entire wagon. Nihla claims he's "sick as a dog." "We hate that guy." (Whether he is older or younger is not yet confirmed.)
- **Hildy Bunce**: Osric's neighbour in Bree · Session 1 [00:29:15] · Talks for an hour about things that should take thirty seconds, like missing vegetables. · *heard as:* Hildebunst
- **Falco Goodbody**: hobbit, Shire delegate to the Council of Bree · Session 1 [01:01:55] · Late middle-aged, skittish, dislikes crowds. Worried about East Road safety; wants patrols; prefers diplomacy.
- **Rowan Heatherthaw**: eldest councillor, of Bree · Session 1 [01:04:28] · Grey-haired, respected, cautious and conservative; thinks the reports exaggerated. · *heard as:* Heatherthau, Heather *(spelling unconfirmed)*
- **Hilda Underbow**: hobbit councillor for Staddle · Session 1 [01:04:28] · Older, sharp one-liners, no patience for foolishness, excellent memory. Submitted the report on abandoned farms. Session 2 [00:40:36]: at the breakfast briefing; sure there are ghosts; gave directions to the abandoned farmstead. Nihla hides from her. · *heard as:* Hufflepuff (table joke), Hildo
- **Tom Brackenford**: councillor for Archet · Session 1 [01:05:32] · Gruff human, former hunter. Submitted the footprint report. · *heard as:* Brackenreed
- **Edric Crowmantle**: councillor from a village near Bree · Session 1 [01:06:00] · Farmer's clothes, but prosperous. Wants local militias; critical of the Rangers. Session 2 [00:29:06]: at the Prancing Pony after the council, working the room; argued with the old knitter ("This is why nobody trusts prophets"). · *heard as:* Chromanto, Yedrick Romandol, Eidric, Idric, Idrik, Crow Mantle, Crow Mental
- **Mayor Coombe**: presides over the Council of Bree · Session 1 [01:20:20] · Shares his name with the village of Combe/Coombe. · *heard as:* Merakum, Miracom
- **The old knitter** (name unknown): white-bearded old man in a woven blue-and-white cloak · Session 1 [00:58:51] · Hid at the council; later knitting a blue scarf with stars by the Prancing Pony's hearth. Speaks in riddles: "All roads are threads…" Session 2: knows some Elven words and recognizes the name Elbereth [00:16:43]; told Edric Crowmantle that history is "an old tapestry" [00:20:15]. The DM revealed his scarf always repeats a pattern of **seven stars** [00:10:50]. Blue fabric like his was found by the raided barrow [02:23:12]; Hanarr thinks he is following them. · *heard as:* old weaver, tricot
- **Keeper of the Bree archives** (name unknown) · Session 1 [01:57:17] · Asked Bill to investigate stolen records quietly.
- **Road warden** (name unknown) · Session 1 [01:22:44] · Brought news that the caravan survivor had died. Not seen at the Prancing Pony afterwards; probably gone home (Session 2 [00:13:32]).
- **The old man with the cart** (name unknown): elderly man with an overturned cart on the Greenway · Session 2 [01:07:01] · Crows attacked as the party helped him. A crude, tattoo-like mark on his hand, seen by Thalion and Osric [01:13:18], which felt to Osric like a brand. Seemed truthful (Nihla's Insight); carried apples and cloth; left down a side trail off the Greenway. · *heard as:* Cartman (table joke)
- **The pale figure** (unknown): seen crossing a far field near the abandoned farmstead with a floating gait; vanished beyond a ridge · Session 2 [01:51:30]
- **The blonde elf-woman** (name unknown): seen only in a vision · Session 2 [02:23:42] · Blonde hair, brown cloak, holding blue fabric like a handkerchief. Came into Hanarr's mind when he took the blue fabric by the barrow. Her description struck Thalion as familiar [02:28:26].
- **The barrow spirit** (name unknown): flickering armoured noble carrying a flag of old Arnor · Session 2 [02:40:56] · Neither hostile nor friendly. Showed visions of a city of Arnor and its fall, and the seven stars; pointed to the hidden lead tube; spoke in broken, old Westron: "Too late… They search again," and pointed west to the Barrow-downs. The DM suggested another power may be sending the image. Buried with old rusted armour and a huge decorated sword. · *heard as:* Mr. Demartine
- **Young hobbit server**: helps Barliman at the Prancing Pony; served breakfast · Session 2 [00:37:45]
- **Red-nosed regular**: Prancing Pony regular pickpocketed by Osric · Session 2 [00:32:30]

## Campaign places

- **Bree**: Session 1 · Includes the Council Hall [00:34:08], the Greenway House [01:35:30] and the Prancing Pony [02:20:49]. Many hobbits live there. · *heard as:* Brie, Bri
- **Council Hall (Bree)**: Session 1 [00:34:08] · Half-round council chamber, eight council seats, a high chair for the Mayor, a public lobby behind a gate, side rooms.
- **Greenway House / Greenway Trading House**: Session 1 [01:52:29] · Bill's headquarters across the street from the Council Hall. The public lobby is oddly small for such a large building. Storerooms, a counting-room, Bill's private office, and an upper floor.
- **Prancing Pony**: Bree inn run by Barliman Butterbur · Session 1 [00:34:08] · Session 2: a corner parlour with divans [00:39:33]; stables next door with 7–8 horses and 4–5 ponies [00:48:47]. · *heard as:* Pantsy Pony, frantic pony, Frontier Pony, forensic pony, printing cloney
- **Longbottom, Southfarthing**: home of the Hornblowers · Session 1 [00:10:33] · *heard as:* "itself farthing"
- **Hopsbury**: where Nihla's uncle lives (mail keeps going to the Hornblowers by mistake) · Session 1 [00:15:38]
- **Michel Delving**: shipments destination for the Hornblowers · Session 1 [00:13:10] · *heard as:* Mikkel Delving, Mikhail Delvin, Michael Delving
- **Archet**: village north of Bree; missing livestock and giant footprints north of it · Session 1 [01:05:32] · *heard as:* Archit, Archeit, Archef, Archette, Arche
- **Staddle**: village near Bree; Hilda Underbow's town · Session 1 [01:05:32]
- **Weather Hills**: source of the footprint report · Session 1 [01:09:16]
- **The Greenway**: trade road where Greenway caravans have been attacked · Session 1 [01:10:41] · Session 2 [00:54:30]: a rough but majestic trail of roots and grass under arching trees, rougher than the East Road; open fields and isolated homesteads along it. · *heard as:* Greenaway, green way
- **Mount Gram**: far north; orcs reported in hills south of it · Session 1 [01:55:50] · *heard as:* Mount Graham
- **Barrow-downs (former lands of Cardolan)**: haunted lands where families fled · Session 1 [02:28:58] · Session 2 [02:57:17]: the barrow spirit pointed west toward them; **the party's next destination**. · *heard as:* Barrowlands, Bearlands, bearer downs, barrel end, Barrow Hills, Bear Downs, barrel downs
- **East Road**: Hanarr's ambush; runs to the Shire · Session 1 [00:01:52]
- **High Pass; Rivendell**: Thalion's route west · Session 1 [00:21:03] · Thalion says Rivendell is a place of learning (music, arts, stars, history) where he never heard of a theft (Session 2 [00:58:52]). · *heard as:* Eurydor (for Eriador); Rhythmia, Ribbendale (Rivendell)
- **Blue Mountains**: Hanarr's home · Session 1 [00:45:00]
- **Thistlewool tailoring shop**: mentioned by Osric at the Prancing Pony · Session 1 [02:37:02] · Shares Osric's surname.
- **Bree, south quarter and south gate**: Session 2 [00:22:28] · Each of Bree's four gates (north, south, east, west) has two guardhouses. The south quarter is poorer and livelier at night than the centre [00:23:15]. Thalion kept watch from a south gate tower.
- **The abandoned farmstead**: Session 2 [01:46:41] · On the Greenway, 15–18 miles from Bree, a day's ride [00:46:30]; the farm from Hilda Underbow's report. Its family fled; meals left to mould, toys on the floor, no sign of violence [01:49:45]. Fresh footprints lead to the rolling hills west of the fields.
- **The raided barrow**: Session 2 [02:37:09] · In the hills 120–150 feet beyond the farmstead's woods; guarded by floating lights. Door forced, lock broken, earth dug, torch soot; burial goods untouched. A chamber about 10 feet wide and 20–30 feet deep, with a tomb (skeleton, rusted armour, a huge decorated sword). A lead tube was hidden behind a loose stone. · *heard as:* barrel
- **Arnor**: the old northern kingdom; the barrow spirit's flag is Arnorian · Session 2 [02:45:15] · Split into **Arthedain**, **Cardolan** and **Rhudaur**; the Witch-king of **Angmar** took Rhudaur. The raided barrow lies on the outskirts of Cardolan and Arthedain [02:47:00]. · *heard as:* Arthur 9, Arthedine, Arthedyn (Arthedain); Cardinal (Cardolan); Rudaur, Roodeaur (Rhudaur); Agmar (Angmar)
