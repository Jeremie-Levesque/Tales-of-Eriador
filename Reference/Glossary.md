## Campaign NPCs

- **Bill (William) Butterbur**: Bree merchant, head of the Greenway Trading Company, councilman; the party's employer · Session 1 [00:01:52] · Well respected; nearly all trade in and out of Bree goes through his company. A contact of the Rangers. Paid the party a 20 gp retainer each; seems to know more than he says. Brother of Barliman. · *heard as:* Butterburry, Butterberg, Butterburk, Butterbird
- **Barliman Butterbur**: keeper of the Prancing Pony; Bill's brother · Session 1 [00:54:15] · Housed the party for the night. · *heard as:* Barleman, Barleyman, Barlamen, Barlin
- **Aldrin, son of Borin**: Ranger · Session 1 [00:22:18] · Thalion's teacher, companion and friend. Declined Bill's invitation and sent Thalion in his place. · *heard as:* Aldrin, son of Lorne
- **Odo Hornblower**: Nihla's brother · Session 1 [00:13:24] · Chosen by the family for the Bree business; once lost an entire wagon. Nihla claims he's "sick as a dog." "We hate that guy." (Whether he is older or younger is not yet confirmed.)
- **Hildy Bunce**: Osric's neighbour in Bree · Session 1 [00:29:15] · Talks for an hour about things that should take thirty seconds, like missing vegetables. · *heard as:* Hildebunst
- **Falco Goodbody**: hobbit, Shire delegate to the Council of Bree · Session 1 [01:01:55] · Late middle-aged, skittish, dislikes crowds. Worried about East Road safety; wants patrols; prefers diplomacy.
- **Rowan Heatherthaw**: eldest councillor, of Bree · Session 1 [01:04:28] · Grey-haired, respected, cautious and conservative; thinks the reports exaggerated. · *heard as:* Heatherthau, Heather *(spelling unconfirmed)*
- **Hilda Underbow**: hobbit councillor for Staddle · Session 1 [01:04:28] · Older, sharp one-liners, no patience for foolishness, excellent memory. Submitted the report on abandoned farms. · *heard as:* Hufflepuff (table joke)
- **Tom Brackenford**: councillor for Archet · Session 1 [01:05:32] · Gruff human, former hunter. Submitted the footprint report. · *heard as:* Brackenreed
- **Edric Crowmantle**: councillor from a village near Bree · Session 1 [01:06:00] · Farmer's clothes, but prosperous. Wants local militias; critical of the Rangers. · *heard as:* Chromanto
- **Mayor Coombe**: presides over the Council of Bree · Session 1 [01:20:20] · Shares his name with the village of Combe/Coombe. · *heard as:* Merakum
- **The old knitter** (name unknown): white-bearded old man in a woven blue-and-white cloak · Session 1 [00:58:51] · Hid at the council; later knitting a blue scarf with stars by the Prancing Pony's hearth. Speaks in riddles: "All roads are threads…"
- **Keeper of the Bree archives** (name unknown) · Session 1 [01:57:17] · Asked Bill to investigate stolen records quietly.
- **Road warden** (name unknown) · Session 1 [01:22:44] · Brought news that the caravan survivor had died.
