*(To read aloud at the start of Session 02. About 220 words.)*

Previously, on Tales of Eriador…

Four strangers were summoned to Bree by letters bearing the seal of the Greenway Trading Company. [00:01:52] **Hanarr**, a dwarf caravan guard, broke a brigand ambush in a battle-rage and caught merchant Bill Butterbur's eye. [00:15:38] **Nihla Hornblower** stole a letter meant for her family and slipped away from the Southfarthing. [00:24:27] **Thalion**, freed after eight years of captivity, was sent by his ranger mentor Aldrin to find answers in Bree. [00:31:19] And **Osric Thistlewool** found a mysteriously dry letter on his doorstep.

[01:08:08] At the Council of Bree they heard of giant footprints and missing sheep near Archet, harried caravans on the Greenway, and families fleeing lights among the barrows. [01:21:24] An unseen voice warned that the wild has grown restless. [01:22:44] Then a road warden brought word that a caravan's last survivor had died repeating two words: *seeing* and *listening*.

[01:26:00] Bill paid each of them twenty gold to find the truth. [01:57:17] He also revealed that records had been stolen from the Bree archives. [02:07:59] Thalion wanted to hunt orcs in the east, but the company chose the haunted south first.

[02:31:29] That night at the Prancing Pony, a blue-cloaked old man knitting a scarf of stars told them only this: "All roads are threads…"

In the morning, the road leads to the Barrow-downs.
