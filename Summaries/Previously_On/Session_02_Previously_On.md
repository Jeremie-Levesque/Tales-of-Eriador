*(To read aloud at the start of Session 03. About 230 words.)*

Previously, on Tales of Eriador…

[00:15:05] At the Prancing Pony, **Thalion** greeted the old knitter in Elvish. The old man knew only a few Elven words, but he knew the name of Elbereth. [00:20:15] **Osric** overheard him tell councillor Edric Crowmantle that history is an old tapestry whose threads fade, are cut, or are forgotten.

[00:39:00] At dawn, Bill Butterbur and Hilda Underbow sent the company down the Greenway on borrowed mounts, bound for an abandoned farmstead. [01:04:01] A dozen crows shadowed them, seeing and listening. [01:14:20] At a fallen cart the crows attacked. **Hanarr** raged, and **Nihla Hornblower** loosed a burst of pale starlight that even she couldn't explain. The cart's old driver bore a strange mark on his hand.

[01:49:45] The farmhouse stood as if its family had vanished mid-meal. [02:00:10] After dark, floating lights guarding a barrow attacked and were destroyed. [02:23:02] Nearby, Hanarr found a scrap of blue fabric and saw a blonde elf-woman in his mind, a face Thalion felt he knew.

[02:40:56] Inside the forced barrow, a flickering spirit showed them a city of old Arnor and seven stars: the very stars on the Greenway Trading Company's seal. [02:55:40] It led them to a hidden parchment: "…the eyes must not fall into northern hands… the stars shall guide them." [02:56:45] Then it spoke: "Too late. They search again," and pointed west, to the Barrow-downs.

It is midnight, and Osric sleeps alone at the farmhouse.
