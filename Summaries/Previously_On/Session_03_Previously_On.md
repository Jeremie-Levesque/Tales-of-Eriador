*(To read aloud at the start of Session 04. About 240 words.)*

Previously, on Tales of Eriador…

[00:12:05] Back at the farmhouse at midnight, **Hanarr**, **Thalion** and **Nihla Hornblower** found **Osric** asleep under a pile of dolls, their faces turned toward him. Nobody could say who put them there. Thalion watched all night, and the dolls never moved.

[00:27:31] In the dark, Nihla followed a familiar tug outside to a dark-haired woman whose hair shone like tiny stars. "Show me the road," the woman said. "I cannot see it." She named herself Varda, Queen of the Stars, and vanished.

[00:51:44] West across the downs, the company found a greater barrow with seven stars over its door, and inside it four men with crowbars, "scholars from Tharbad." [01:04:16] Their leader claimed that councillor Crowmantle works for them. [01:18:20] When he cracked open a tomb, three specters burst out and went for the raiders, and the black mark of a brotherhood spread across his hand.

[01:59:48] The company subdued and bound the raiders. [02:25:12] The leader confessed: Father Corwyn had sent them to find a key in the barrow of seven stars.

[02:37:25] Behind a secret door, Thalion and Nihla set seven stars on an altar, and a noble's tomb opened: a crown, a star sapphire, a glowing scimitar, a strange longsword, and a brass key. [02:56:20] A second parchment spoke of a seeing stone, an eye entrusted to the house of the wardens, and seven stars concealing the road.

Three prisoners, one key, and two days' walk to Bree.
