### Hanarr (Dwarf Barbarian, Jeremie)
- [00:03:35] Caravan guard for Bill Butterbur, from the Blue Mountains [00:45:00]. Carries a huge battle-axe and a maul, wears a leather pelt [01:37:02], long beard and hair going grey. Spent most of his life "inside a mountain" [00:39:04].
- [00:04:33] Broke the brigand ambush with a rage. [00:07:36] Deflected Bill's questions about it.
- [00:08:10] Received Bill's council invitation.
- [00:36:44] Politely greeted Nihla ("Master Hobbit") and asked what everyone did for a living.
- [00:58:51] **Spotted the hidden old knitter** at the council and warned Bill. [01:22:10] Noticed a hooded road warden while looking for the source of the warning.
- [01:24:15] Stayed on guard beside Bill during the uproar.
- [01:27:09] Asked Bill what "resolved" would look like. "20 gold is quite generous." [01:31:05]
- [01:41:35] Went straight to the Greenway House and waited for the others ("Nice of you to show up").
- [02:03:39] Felt an unexplained **pull toward the haunted lands**, "like an itch or a tickle," and cast the deciding vote for the south.
- [02:11:14] Sensed Bill knows more than he says (Insight 15).
- [02:23:00] Found common ground with Thalion: "your bloodlust for orcs is quite refreshing."
- [02:33:30] **Confronted the old knitter** at the Prancing Pony, got riddles, and left with a warning.
- **Gained:** 20 gp retainer [01:26:00].

### Thalion (Elf, Dominic)
- [00:21:31] About seven feet tall, golden-haired, scarred, in chainmail with a curved longsword, shield and an ugly bascinet.
- [00:24:21] Spent eight years in captivity and six months home; travels with his mentor Aldrin, son of Borin, who sent him to Bree for answers.
- [00:43:45] Introduced himself to Nihla as "Thalion, of Rivendell."
- [00:49:15] Tuned his lute badly: he sings in tune, but his chords are off.
- [01:02:46] Puzzled why "children" (hobbits) sit on the council.
- [01:29:19] Suspected Bill of protecting his profits.
- [01:39:15] **Natural 20 on Perception.** Heard everything.
- [01:47:00] Tore the knob off Nihla's pot-lid shield.
- [01:49:36] Decided Aldrin sent him to learn something, perhaps from a hobbit. [01:52:15] Declined Nihla's lute lessons.
- [02:07:59] Argued hard to go **east after the orcs** and was outvoted. Told Bill: "This is not a ghost story." [02:10:22]
- [02:18:30] Came around after Nihla's heartfelt appeal: "I must learn first."
- [02:41:15] Told the old knitter: "We are about to change things around here. Might as well tell us what you know." Noticed stars on his scarf.
- **Gained:** 20 gp retainer [01:26:00].

### Nihla Hornblower (Halfling Cleric, Alex)
- [00:11:58] A young-looking adult hobbit from the Hornblower pipe-weed family of Longbottom. Brown hair, brown eyes, practical trousers, commanding and forceful.
- [00:16:14] **Took the family's letter from Bree** and left home to represent the Hornblowers herself, instead of her brother Odo.
- [00:40:15] Natural 1 bragging about the family; handed Osric a leaf sample.
- [00:42:45] Bristled at Thalion's stare, then was struck by his beauty and his sorrow.
- [00:51:48] Told Bill that Odo was sick and Pa had a broken leg.
- [00:53:26] Pocketed leftover food.
- [01:18:18] Scorned the cautious councillors: "Bureaucrats!"
- [01:27:40] Second natural 1 in a row.
- [01:45:53] **Bought hobbit-sized scale mail** and got a pot-lid shield for free.
- [01:51:08] Criticized Thalion's lute technique and offered lessons.
- [02:06:34] Sided with Hanarr's pull south: "Wherever you're going, Master Dwarf, I think that's the path for me too."
- [02:18:30] Won Thalion over by wishing on a star and believing really hard (Persuasion 12).
- [02:25:58] Showed off her scar, which she hints didn't come from a bounder. [02:28:21] Read Osric (Insight 25): grey side of the law.
- [02:41:56] Wrote home, sent some gold, and bought her own lute.
- **Gained:** [01:26:00] 20 gp; [01:45:53] scale mail; [01:46:15] pot-lid shield; [02:42:56] a lute; [00:53:26] pocketed food.

### Osric Thistlewool (Human Rogue, Benoit)
- [00:27:47] Lives on the southern edge of Bree; long brown hair, scar on his forehead, scimitar; likes learning his neighbours' secrets.
- [00:31:19] Found a dry letter on his doorstep, delivered by no one he saw.
- [00:40:28] Accepted a Hornblower leaf sample.
- [00:54:15] Mixed up Bill and Barliman, and asked Bill directly why he'd been invited.
- [01:15:15] **Relaxed when none of the reports concerned him.**
- [01:18:30] Sided with diplomacy: "fewer swords on the road."
- [01:26:37] Took the retainer: "My fees are usually lower."
- [01:40:15] Went home for his tools. [01:48:15] **Gained 3 Shadow points** after a failed Wisdom save tied to his past.
- [02:09:02] Backed going south: "It seems three of us are going south."
- [02:20:39] Arranged rooms at the Prancing Pony through Bill.
- [02:24:55] Admits to "a little danger" in his past. Nihla's Insight suggests he's on the grey side of the law [02:28:21].
- [02:44:15] Checks his belongings every night in case he's been robbed.
- **Gained:** [01:26:00] 20 gp retainer; [01:48:42] 3 Shadow points.
