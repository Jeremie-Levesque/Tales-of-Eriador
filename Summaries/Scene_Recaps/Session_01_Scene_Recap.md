### Scene 1: The ambush on the East Road [00:01:52–00:10:33]
A week before the Council of Bree, brigands ambushed a Greenway caravan loaded with Hornblower leaf. **Hanarr**, the dwarf guarding merchant Bill Butterbur, flew into a rage and charged. The half-starved attackers broke and fled, and a few surrendered. At the campfire Bill asked Hanarr straight out what had happened. Hanarr deflected (a lone dwarf does what he must), and Bill handed him a sealed invitation to the council, because he wanted people "who've seen things."
- **NPCs:** Bill Butterbur [00:01:52]
- **Rolls:** Insight 11 [00:07:16]; History 11 [00:09:07]
- **Items:** the sealed letter [00:08:10]

### Scene 2: A letter for the Hornblowers [00:10:33–00:21:03]
In Longbottom, **Nihla** listened to her family choose her careless brother Odo to handle business in Bree. When the postman brought a letter from Bree, she pocketed it, read it in secret, decided to represent the family herself, and hitched a wagon ride out of the Southfarthing.
- **NPCs:** Odo Hornblower, Nihla's father and brothers, the postman [00:13:10–00:15:38]
- **Rolls:** Sleight of Hand to hide the letter [00:16:45]

### Scene 3: Aldrin's lesson [00:21:03–00:27:30]
West of the High Pass, **Thalion** and his mentor Aldrin, son of Borin, were overtaken by a ranger messenger carrying Bill's letter. Aldrin gave it to Thalion, who had spent eight years in captivity and six months looking for answers, and sent him to Bree to "listen, watch, ask questions."
- **NPCs:** Aldrin, son of Borin; an unnamed ranger messenger [00:22:18]

### Scene 4: The dry letter [00:27:30–00:32:54]
On a rainy night in Bree, **Osric** heard someone at his door, found the lane empty, and found a dry letter waiting on his doorstep, addressed to him on expensive paper.
- **NPCs:** Hildy Bunce (mentioned) [00:29:15]
- **Rolls:** Perception 20 [00:30:11]; Stealth [00:30:41]

### Scene 5: Strangers in the side room [00:32:54–00:57:15]
Shown into a private room at the Council Hall, the four sized each other up. Nihla bristled at being stared at, boasted about the family business, and fumbled it (natural 1). She gave Osric a sample of Hornblower leaf and was dazzled, then saddened, by Thalion. Thalion tuned his lute badly. Bill arrived, greeted each of them, and gave Osric his reason: trouble is brewing, and he wants people willing to get their hands dirty.
- **Places:** Council Hall, the Greenway Trading Company (explained) [00:47:14]
- **Rolls:** Nihla Deception natural 1 [00:40:15]; Hanarr History 3 [00:39:18]

### Scene 6: The Council of Bree [00:57:15–01:25:00]
Seated behind Bill, the party watched five councillors and Mayor Coombe hear three reports: giant footprints and missing sheep in the Weather Hills, attacks on Greenway caravans, and families abandoning farms near lit-up barrows. Hanarr spotted a hiding old man with a white beard, knitting. The council deadlocked. An unseen voice warned that "the wild has grown restless." Then a road warden burst in: a caravan survivor had died repeating "Seeing… listening."
- **NPCs:** Falco Goodbody, Rowan Heatherthaw, Hilda Underbow, Tom Brackenford, Edric Crowmantle, Mayor Coombe, the old knitter, the road warden [00:58:51–01:22:44]
- **Clues:** [01:09:16] the footprint sketch; [01:10:41] "organized scouting"; [01:13:13] lights among the barrows; [01:22:44] "Seeing… listening"

### Scene 7: Bill's retainer [01:25:00–01:35:50]
Bill paid each of them 20 gold to find out what the council couldn't. The party sensed he was operating outside the council and knew more than he said, and Thalion suspected he was protecting his profits. Bill asked them to come to the Greenway House that evening.
- **Loot:** 20 gp each [01:26:00]

### Scene 8: Pot lids and lute lessons [01:35:50–01:52:29]
Nihla bought hobbit-sized scale mail and talked the smith into giving her a pot-lid shield, whose knob Thalion promptly tore off. Thalion, trailing after her, decided Aldrin must have sent him to learn something, perhaps from a hobbit, and refused her offer of lute lessons. Off on his own, Osric gained 3 Shadow points.
- **Loot:** scale mail, pot-lid shield [01:45:53–01:46:43]
- **Rolls:** Thalion Perception natural 20 [01:39:15]; Osric Wisdom save 6, 3 Shadow points [01:48:15]

### Scene 9: Five leads and a vote [01:52:29–02:20:19]
In his office, Bill laid out the leads, including stolen records from the Bree archives. Hanarr felt drawn to the haunted lands, and Nihla and Osric sided with him. Thalion argued hard for chasing the orcs east but was outvoted three to one. Bill suggested doing both, south first. Nihla wished on a star and won Thalion over.
- **Rolls:** Insight on Bill 15/11 [02:11:22]; Hanarr Persuasion 7 [02:17:58]; Nihla Persuasion 12 [02:18:30]

### Scene 10: The walk to the Pony [02:20:19–02:30:33]
Bill arranged rooms with his brother Barliman. Hanarr and Thalion found common ground in hating orcs. Nihla showed off her scar and read Osric (25): he lives on the grey edge of the law. Bill sent them off toward the Barrow-downs.

### Scene 11: The knitter by the hearth [02:30:33–02:44:45]
At the Prancing Pony, Hanarr confronted the blue-cloaked old man, who answered only in riddles: "All roads are threads…" Thalion sensed he knew a great deal and demanded he share it. Nihla wrote home, reluctantly sent some gold, and bought a lute. Everyone turned in.
