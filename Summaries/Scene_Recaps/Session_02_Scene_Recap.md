### Scene 1: Elvish words for the knitter [00:11:23–00:17:14]
The night was still young at the Prancing Pony, and the common room was busy with regulars. **Hanarr** and **Nihla** went up to their rooms (each had a room of their own). **Thalion** asked after the wounded road warden, but he wasn't there and had probably gone home. Thalion then sat beside the old knitter and murmured the Elvish greeting *Elen síla lúmenn' omentielvo* ("a star shines on the hour of our meeting") to see whether it stirred anything. "Master Elf," the old man replied, he knew some Elven words but not the whole language. Had Thalion said something about stars? Stars, he said, shine a light upon the road and give us what we need when we need it. When Thalion asked whether he knew Elbereth, he said he recognized the name. Thalion left him with a polite goodbye and walked out into the night.
- **NPCs:** the old knitter [00:15:04]; the road warden (absent) [00:13:32]
- **Clues:** [00:10:50] (before play resumed) the DM revealed that the knitter's scarf always repeats a pattern of **seven stars**; only three showed in the picture shared after Session 01. [00:16:43] The knitter recognizes the name Elbereth.

### Scene 2: Thalion's watch over the south gate [00:21:22–00:28:40, 00:35:44–00:37:30]
At about nine o'clock, Thalion walked Bree after nightfall, troubled that the threat seemed to be inside the walls too: records had been stolen from the archives. He didn't notice much (Perception 6), but he did see that the south of town was poorer and livelier at night than the centre. Each of Bree's gates has two guardhouses. At the south gate, Thalion traded nods with the watchman, climbed the tower and looked south over the Greenway, a road far wilder and rougher than the East Road. He thought about Hilda Underbow: she was no fool, so her report must hold something. He also remembered Aldrin's lesson that "answers rarely wait where we left them." He admitted to himself that he was out of his element among hobbits and men, and that rushing back to what he knew wasn't working. He kept watch from the tower until sunrise.
- **Places:** Bree's south quarter; the south gate and its guardhouses [00:22:28]; the view down the Greenway [00:25:15]
- **Rolls:** Thalion Perception 6 [00:23:00]
- **Clues:** [00:37:00] Having walked the south quarter at night and in the morning, Thalion may notice anomalies there next time.

### Scene 3: Osric works the common room [00:17:30–00:34:22]
**Osric** stayed downstairs. After Thalion left, a patron sat down with the knitter as if picking up an old conversation. Osric recognized him (Perception 13): Edric Crowmantle, the prosperous "farmer" councillor. The knitter told him the history books are full of contradictions, because history is "like an old tapestry. Sometimes threads fade, sometimes threads are cut, and sometimes everyone forgets they ever were there." Edric snapped, "Again with your riddles!" and told him he wasn't from around here. "The world changes, roads move, kingdoms fall," the old man answered. "Sometimes people stay in one place so long they mistake it for permanence." Edric shook his head ("This is why nobody trusts prophets"), went back to the bar and later left. Osric read him as a politician working the room after the council (Insight 10), which made Osric uneasy, since a militia is bad for people like him. Then Osric picked the pocket of a red-nosed regular (Sleight of Hand, success) and went up to bed. On the way, he tied the man's old handkerchief to Nihla's door and left sandwich crumbs on the threshold.
- **NPCs:** Edric Crowmantle [00:29:06]; the old knitter [00:20:02]; a red-nosed regular [00:32:30]
- **Rolls:** Osric Perception 13 [00:29:01]; Insight 10 [00:31:21]; Sleight of Hand, success [00:33:19]
- **Loot:** a few copper pieces, some knick-knacks and an old handkerchief [00:33:30]

### Scene 4: Breakfast in the parlour [00:37:30–00:47:45]
In the morning, a young hobbit who helps Barliman served breakfast. Bill Butterbur arrived soon after Thalion came back and asked to talk "without prying ears" in a corner parlour full of divans. He had brought **Hilda Underbow**, who warned them to take the matter seriously: "No laughing matter." Nihla made herself scarce, because the sharp old councillor frightened her. Thalion asked Hanarr why he wanted to go to the barrows. Hanarr said that lately he was more interested in mysterious, unknown forces than in straightforward enemies. Hilda was sure there were ghosts among "these big mounds of dead things." Thalion was less convinced, but he smiled, nodded and found her bluntness endearing. Since the Greenway is too rough for a wagon, Bill offered to lend them horses and ponies through his brother Barliman. Hanarr asked what a successful resolution would look like. Thalion pointed out that nobody yet knew what the problem was. Hilda gave them directions to the farmstead where a family had fled. It lies 15 to 18 miles from Bree, a day's ride at a steady pace, so they would be gone at least two days.
- **NPCs:** Bill Butterbur [00:38:55]; Hilda Underbow [00:40:36]; Barliman Butterbur (mentioned) [00:44:34]; a young hobbit server [00:37:45]
- **Places:** the parlour at the Prancing Pony [00:39:33]; the abandoned farmstead (directions) [00:45:45]

### Scene 5: Ponies and the south gate [00:47:45–00:55:34]
Osric excused himself to run errands, promising to meet them at the south gate. ("Make them quick," said Hanarr.) Barliman took the others to the stables beside the Pony. They borrowed two horses and two ponies at no charge: **Reginald**, a brown horse, for Thalion; **Epona**, a pony, for Hanarr; **Maxwell**, a horse, for Osric; and **Fitzgerald**, a pony, for Nihla. Osric didn't reach the gate first (a roll of 10). At about 7:30, rather than be seen with them, he walked straight past the guards like a stranger (Deception, success), so as not to ruin his bad reputation. He fell back in with the others once Bree's walls were out of sight. The Greenway was a rough but majestic trail of roots and grass under arching trees, passing open fields and isolated homesteads outside any village.
- **Items:** four borrowed mounts [00:49:51–00:50:15]
- **Rolls:** Osric 10 [00:51:15]; Osric Deception, success [00:52:28]
- **Places:** the stables of the Prancing Pony [00:48:47]; the Greenway [00:53:54]

### Scene 6: No thieves in Rivendell [00:55:34–01:00:15]
On the road, Thalion asked Osric whether thefts like the one from the archives were normal in Bree. Osric said people always try, but a crime this big doesn't usually happen. Thalion admitted he'd never heard of a theft inside a city at all: he had thought the thugs were outside and order was within. "A thievery well done is never discovered before it's too late," said Osric. Hanarr asked whether Thalion's elf friends had never hidden his helmets as a prank, and Thalion looked down at his one helmet, baffled. In Rivendell, he said, they learn music, arts, stars and history, and they sing and dance. Osric teased him about his lute playing. Thalion conceded that his learning "got somewhat disturbed" and that he had a lot left to learn. "We all have learning to do," said Osric. "I want to learn about these ghosts."

### Scene 7: Seeing and listening [01:00:15–01:06:50]
Everyone noticed a crow off the left side of the road. On reflection (Insight checks of 22, 21, 19 and 15), they realized it wasn't the first crow they had seen: the birds had been following them. Osric, who grew up nearby, said crows around Bree are normally nothing to remark on. Hanarr announced that he felt followed and asked whether anyone knew how to cook crow. Once the party showed they had noticed, a dozen crows openly surrounded them, hopping from branch to branch. "They seem to be seeing and listening," said Thalion. He fired a pebble from his sling at disadvantage and missed. The crows scattered, still audible but out of sight, and at Osric's suggestion the party rode hard for half an hour to lose them.
- **Clues:** [01:04:39] the crows are "seeing and listening," echoing the dying survivor's last words
- **Rolls:** Insight 22, 21, 19 and 15 [01:02:38]; Thalion's sling, miss [01:05:53]

### Scene 8: The fallen cart [01:06:50–01:37:30]
They came upon an elderly man beside an overturned cart. Osric smelled an ambush but saw nothing (Perception natural 1). Hanarr readied his greataxe and put on his helmet. Thalion dismounted and offered help, and his Insight (natural 1) found nothing amiss. As they helped right the cart, **Thalion** and **Osric** spotted a crude, tattoo-like mark on the old man's hand. Then six crows dived in. Hanarr roared, "It's about time!", flew into a rage and burst a crow apart with one blow (23 to hit, 18 slashing). Nihla clutched the star medallion at her neck, closed her eyes and threw out her hands. A blinding burst of pale, pinkish-white light, like starlight or moonlight, poured from her, and the crows shrieked. Osric had never seen anything like it. Hanarr was more curious than alarmed. Thalion sensed (Insight 15) that the power felt Elvish, like a glimpse of a mighty elf's true strength, and couldn't understand how it came from a hobbit. The crows pecked back, though only Nihla was scratched (1 damage), and their flapping distracted Osric (failed Wisdom save). The party then cut them down one by one. Osric struck with his scimitar for 12, and Thalion speared a crow hiding between his legs. Hanarr swatted one to the ground with the flat of his axe, Nihla killed one with her mace, and Osric finished the last with his short sword. They were ordinary birds.
- **NPCs:** the old man with the cart [01:07:01]
- **Clues:** [01:13:18–01:14:00] a crude, tattoo-like design on the old man's hand (the DM shared a sketch)
- **Rolls:** Osric Perception **natural 1** [01:07:27]; Thalion Insight **natural 1** [01:08:31]; initiative Hanarr 23, Nihla 20, crows 19, Osric 18, Thalion 15 [01:10:35–01:11:19]; Hanarr 23 to hit, 18 damage [01:15:31]; Thalion Insight 15 on Nihla's light [01:23:15]; Osric 12 damage [01:26:55]

### Scene 9: The man with the mark [01:37:30–01:46:24]
Osric put his blade to the old man's throat: "I'm sure you haven't organized this alone. Tell them to come out if you want your life spared." "Easy now," said Thalion. Hanarr, still raging, hacked at the trees, shouting at anything hiding there to come out. The old man apologized for the "strange behaviour from birds," wished them a good day and led his cart off the Greenway down a side trail. His mark was now hidden under his clothing. His cart held only apples and cloth. Nihla judged he was telling the truth (Insight 8), and Osric sheathed his weapon, though the mark still felt to him like a brand. Hanarr asked whether they were going to kill a reasonable man, and Osric replied that he knew human ways and this looked like an ambush. Human behaviour left Thalion bewildered. Afterwards, Hanarr asked Nihla about her light. She said it was her first time swinging a mace and killing a bird, and that the light had shocked her too. Hanarr's Insight suggested a half-truth, and Osric pressed her for "the whole truth." Nihla shot back that he might get further if he asked nicely instead of calling a girl weird. Didn't his mama teach him manners?
- **Rolls:** Nihla Insight 8 on the old man [01:41:30]; Hanarr Insight on Nihla [01:44:44]
- **Items:** the old man's cart: apples and cloth [01:43:00]

### Scene 10: The abandoned farmstead [01:46:24–01:58:21]
In the late afternoon, beyond a line of hills, they found the farmstead Hilda had described. The gate was unlocked and the horses were calm. Osric still suspected a children's prank, and Thalion watched for wind chimes in the trees. A careful look (one roll of 22) showed nothing amiss in the empty grazing fields. Inside the house, half-eaten meals had gone mouldy on the table and children's toys lay on the floor. There was no sign of violence, as if life had simply stopped. As the sun went down, Hanarr and Nihla, searching the house, saw through a window a pale figure crossing a far field with a floating, unnatural motion. Hanarr ran out, but it vanished beyond a ridge. He found fresh footprints leading toward the rolling hills to the west. (He tried his Stonecunning, but the field was dirt, not stone.) Osric wanted to wait for morning, since men don't see well in the dark. Thalion reminded him that solving it tonight meant getting paid sooner and handed him a torch. "Money talks," Osric conceded. Then, ahead among the trees, they saw three lights.
- **Places:** the abandoned farmstead [01:46:41]
- **Clues:** [01:49:45] the abandoned meal and toys; [01:51:30] a pale figure with a floating gait; [01:54:29] fresh footprints heading west

### Scene 11: Lights in the dark [01:58:21–02:24:53]
The DM allowed a short rest taken earlier on horseback, during which Nihla sang a song of encouragement and gave Thalion and Hanarr Heroic Inspiration. Osric put out his torch to sneak ahead. "Halt!" Hanarr bellowed. "We've been sent by the Bree Council! We need to question you!" The lights swelled and drifted closer with a floating motion, whispering, and turned out to be nothing but floating lights. Thalion, first in the order, ordered them to stand down. He charged, frightening one, and struck another, feeling a jolt like an electrical pulse as it flickered. Hanarr, unsure what to be angry at, raged anyway ("The lights must die!"). He destroyed the wounded light with a thrown hand axe, and it burst with a pop that Thalion dodged. Osric, unnerved at last ("not a kids' prank"), missed everything, and Nihla hid behind Thalion's legs. A bolt from one light hit Osric in the chest for 7 damage, and the lights kept fading, which made them harder to hit. Thalion turned another one red. Osric used his Heroic Inspiration to dodge a burst and then destroyed the last light with an aimed shortbow shot for 17. With his darkvision (Perception 24), Hanarr saw that the lights had been guarding a barrow, whose door stood open. Nearby, he found a scrap of **blue fabric** caught on a rock. As he took it, an image came into his head: a blonde elf in a brown cloak, holding blue fabric like a handkerchief.
- **Clues:** [02:22:05] the lights were guarding a barrow; [02:23:12] blue fabric on a rock; [02:23:42] the vision of a blonde elf
- **Rolls:** Thalion 18 to hit [02:06:30]; Osric 7 damage taken [02:16:06]; Osric shortbow 17 damage [02:20:30]; Hanarr Perception 24 [02:21:42]

### Scene 12: The third option [02:24:53–02:37:09]
Hanarr was sure the "old weaver" from the Prancing Pony was following them ("I told you he was weird"). Thalion saw that the fabric looked similar to the knitter's, with a glint of light on it. Hanarr described his vision, and the DM added that the elf was a woman. To Thalion (Insight 21), the description struck him as familiar. Osric had had enough: hurt by "floating fire" and unable to land a blow, he went back to the farmhouse alone. Asked to feed the horses, he fed only Maxwell and settled in for a long rest. Hanarr wanted to look inside the open barrow, about 120 to 150 feet away, beyond a strip of woods. He also noted that the strangeness had begun only after sundown. Thalion checked on Nihla. She confessed she had "choked up," and that when she reached for the light again she "grabbed nothing." Thalion suggested that next time she call on Elbereth Gilthoniel, the queen of the stars: "She normally helps." Nihla thought he was talking nonsense. ("There are queens in the stars?") They debated going back to the injured human. Thalion offered to go on alone, and Hanarr said that if anyone was going alone, it was him. Thalion settled it: "The third option seems to be going forward." He raised his sword, which shimmered with a dim light, and called out in Elvish, *"Aurë entuluva!"* ("Day shall come again!").
- **Rolls:** Thalion Insight 21 on the elf's description [02:27:20]

### Scene 13: The barrow and the spirit [02:37:09–03:07:20]
The barrow door had been forced, its lock broken. Fresh footprints, dug earth and torch soot showed that someone had got there first, roughly. Nihla saw (Insight) that the burial goods were untouched, so whoever came hadn't been hunting treasure. The chamber was about 10 feet wide and 20 to 30 feet deep, with a tomb at the far end. A figure flickered in and out of existence like a broken message: an armoured noble carrying a flag. Nihla (Insight 23) felt a presence that was neither hostile nor friendly. Visions followed. A great city in its glory, with human riders bringing sealed documents through its gates, then darkness, flames and war. The flag (History) was of old Arnor, and the DM explained how Arnor split into Arthedain, Cardolan and Rhudaur, and how the Witch-king of Angmar took Rhudaur. The barrow lies on the outskirts of Cardolan and Arthedain. Then the figure touched the wall, and **seven stars** burned into their minds. Nihla realized they were aligned exactly like the stars on the Greenway Trading Company's seal. ("They're everywhere!" said Hanarr.) The spirit pointed to a stone set in the wall. Behind it lay a small, rusted lead tube, which Thalion opened (23). Nihla sensed the intruders had been looking for information, probably this, and missed it. Thalion passed the tube to Nihla for her small, careful hands. She asked "Elbereth, or whoever you are" to make it go well, cast Guidance and drew out a crumbling half-parchment (Sleight of Hand with advantage). She read four half-lines aloud before it fell to dust. The spirit grew agitated and spoke for the first time, in a broken, old-sounding Westron: "Too late." After a long pause: "They search again." It pointed west toward the Barrow-downs and vanished. The intruders' tracks were ordinary human-sized boots heading west. ("Probably not an elf, they don't leave tracks," Hanarr grumbled.) Hanarr levered open the tomb: a skeleton, old rusted armour and a huge, richly decorated sword. Asked what would make a ghost appear, the DM said it might not be the dead man himself but some other power wanting them to see this, and that nothing about it had felt evil. It was midnight. The Barrow-downs lie farther on, and they planned to return to the farmhouse and tell Osric everything.
